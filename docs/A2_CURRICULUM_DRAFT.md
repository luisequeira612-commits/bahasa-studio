# A2 curriculum draft — 3.8.7

Status: **internal draft; not unlocked for learners**.

The A2 scaffold now contains eight sequential units. Units U1–U3 are the existing introductory material; U4–U8 extend the progression with:

- mem- and men- forms in frequent active verbs
- meng- and meny- forms
- Contrast between active meN- and passive di- verbs
- The distinction between the attached passive prefix di- and the separate place preposition di

Each new unit includes four vocabulary entries, eight practice prompts, listening, and a short productive response.

**Release gate:** A2 remains hidden from the learner-facing route, vocabulary index, and SRS until the A1 curriculum and final assessment have passed the project's validation criteria. Structural tests do not replace Indonesian language review by a qualified speaker. B1 and B2 remain scaffolded and are not being presented as complete.
